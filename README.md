# Briefly summarize the DriverPass project. Who was the client? What type of system did they want you to design?

The client for this project was DriverPass. The client wanted us to design a system to better accommodate people/customers who are learning to drive motor vehicles. DriverPass noticed there was a need for a better system in driver education and training. People had been failing their driving tests at the DMV.
DriverPass wanted their customers to be able to register/open accounts and provide necessary information in their accounts. They wanted their customers to be able to schedule/reserve driving lessons from a list of three options. They wanted to enable their customers to modify, cancel, and reschedule lessons if needed as well as take online classes and practice tests in reference to the lessons the customers selected. They also wanted their customers to have the ability to reset login credentials if needed as well.
Three specific DriverPass employees, the executive, the IT officer, and the secretary wanted certain administrative access to assist customers and to make sure the system operated properly. The executive wanted full access, mainly to track every activity/event and to generate necessary reports. The IT officer wanted full administrative access for security and maintenance. The secretary wanted access to schedule/reserve lessons for customers on their behalf. DriverPass wanted this system to connect to the DMV for any updates regarding changes to policies and education material/training.
DriverPass wanted the system to be web-based (supported by web browsers) and wanted the host environment to be cloud-based (servers, etc.). With this, the system could be easily accessible with internet access from multiple devices and they wouldn't have to worry about security and backups as much. They wouldn't have to maintain their own local servers as well.

# How did you interpret the user’s needs and implement them into your system design? Why is it so important to consider the user’s needs when designing?

Analyzing the DriverPass interview transcript, picking out the important information/requirements they wanted and then incorporating those into my design documents and diagrams. It's very important to consider the user's (client's) needs into the design, simply because you (and/or your company) won't have many clients if don't, which will eventually lead to failure. Considering the client's needs is paramount to designing a quality product or system that actually works to solve the client's problem.
